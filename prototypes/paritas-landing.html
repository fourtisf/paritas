      03 · Receipt

      A receipt for every trade.

      Most venues bury their cost inside the price. Paritas prints each line separately, before you sign and again in the fill event on-chain.

      Your price=Share price+Spread × session±Inventory+Fee
      Share price
      The live Chainlink price for the stock, already adjusted for splits and dividends.
      Spread × session
      What the vault charges for taking the other side. It widens outside regular hours, when prices are thinner.
      Inventory
      A nudge of up to 0.15% that keeps the vault balanced. When your trade helps rebalance it, it works in your favour.
      Fee
      A flat 0.02% on every swap, whichever venue fills it.

      A hard price limit. On Tier A markets no fill can settle more than 0.75% from the share price, on any venue. If a feed goes stale, a corporate action is pending or the sequencer is down, quoting stops instead of guessing.

      04 · Hours

      The vault keeps Wall Street's hours.

      Share prices only move when the market does, so the vault follows the same clock. This is the current week in your time zone, your local time.

      Regular hours

      9:30 to 16:00 New York

      Extended

      Spread 1.5 times wider. Largest order drops to 75%.

      4:00 to 9:30 and 16:00 to 20:00

      Overnight

      Spread doubles and the largest order halves.

      20:00 to 4:00, Sunday to Friday

      Weekend

      The vault pauses. Uniswap and makers keep filling.

      06 · Vaults

      07 · Safety

      Promises you can check.

      Each one is enforced by the contracts, not a policy page, and each comes with a way to verify it yourself.

      You keep custody

      Swaps settle from your wallet straight back to your wallet. There is no deposit account to top up.

      Check it: open any fill on Blockscout and follow the transfers.

      Prices stay near the share

      A fill outside the tier's price limit reverts, whichever venue quoted it.

      Check it: the limit lives in the router contract and its invariant tests.

      Withdrawals never pause

      Not by governance, not by the guardian, not during a halt or after a market is retired.

      Check it: the vault's withdraw function has no pause switch.

      Changes wait in public

      Spreads, limits and fees only change after a 48-hour timelock, visible before they apply.

      Check it: queued changes appear on the app's governance timeline.

      The code is open

      Contracts and app are public, and the deployed bytecode is matched to the published source.

      Check it: every contract shows verified source on Blockscout.

      Audited before deposits open

      No vault accepts deposits until an independent audit is finished and published.

      Check it: the reports will be linked from this page.

      08 · FAQ

      Plain answers.

      Something missing? The docs cover every parameter and contract.

      Who can use Paritas?

      Anyone outside the United States and other restricted regions who holds USDG or Stock Tokens in their own wallet. Stock Tokens are not available to US persons.

      What are Stock Tokens?

      Tokens issued by Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited that track the price of a listed share or ETF. They are tokenized debt securities: you get price exposure, not voting rights in the company.

      Why does the vault pause on weekends?

      The stock market is closed, so the Chainlink price stops moving while the news does not. Pausing protects liquidity providers from Monday-morning gaps. Uniswap pools and market makers keep filling your trades in the meantime.

      What does a swap cost?

      The venue's spread plus a 0.02% protocol fee, both on the receipt before you sign. Network fees on Robinhood Chain are usually a fraction of a cent.

      Do I need an account?

      No sign-up and no deposit. Connect a wallet, get a quote, swap, and leave whenever you like.

      Is Paritas part of Robinhood?

      No. Paritas is an independent app built on Robinhood Chain. Robinhood does not operate or endorse it.
